Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version: your progress is private to your profile. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, and nothing is shared unless you turn sharing on yourself.

Who runs this site

positiveLife4me is a small personal wellness site run by Adam for himself, family, and friends. People use it by invitation.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email, when you sign in with Google. The email is used only to recognize you and open your profile. We never see your Google password.
  • What you log on the site: check-offs, workouts, lifts, sleep and body check-ins, gratitude notes, and your settings. This is saved to your profile so it's the same on every device you sign in on.
  • Workouts from Intervals.icu, only for profiles where that connection has been set up.
  • Workout logbook photos, only when you use Scan workout. The photo is sent to Cloudflare's AI service to read the sets and reps, then discarded. Only the numbers you confirm are saved.
  • Your location, only if you choose to share it for the weather, pressure, or sky pages. It stays on your device. The coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast and to BigDataCloud to look up the place name.

Where it's kept

Your information is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site. Your device also keeps a copy so pages load fast and work offline.

Who can see it

Only you. Another profile sees something only if you turn it on in Settings under Sharing (for example, today's progress or your streak). The site's owner can see the names and email addresses of the people who are invited, to manage who can sign in.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of your information.
  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking cookies.
  • No use of your Google account beyond your name and email for sign-in.

Removing your information

You can sign out on any device from Settings. To have your profile and everything in it deleted, email adamswitkow@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Questions

Email adamswitkow@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
